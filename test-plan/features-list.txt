FEATURE: Product list 
Location: Home page (All products)
Fields: None
Actions: Items-per-page selector at the bottom, page navigator arrows, Add to basket button on each card, click a card to open its details
Normal behavior: Products are shown as cards, each with an image, name and price. Changing items per page changes how many cards show.

FEATURE: Product details
Location: Click any product card on the home page
Fields: None
Actions: Close button, expand/collapse the reviews section.
Normal behavior: A popup opens showing the product image, name, description and price, with the reviews section below it. Closing it returns to the product list.
Notes: Does the popup close when clicking outside it? Does the description ever show empty?

FEATURE: Product reviews
Location: Product details popup, in the Reviews section (click the expandable arrow)
Fields: Review text box
Actions:  Expand/collapse the reviews section, Submit and Close button
Normal behavior: The Reviews section shows the number of reviews, and each review shows its author and text. A product with no reviews shows 0. Submitting a review adds it to the list.
Notes: Review box when logged out is  hidden. Is there a character limit on the review text?

FEATURE: Basket 
Location: Add to basket on each product card and top right bar Your Basket icon
Fields: Quantity per item
Actions: Increase/decrease quantity (plus and minus), remove item (trash icon), Checkout button
Normal behavior: Displays any item added with its quantity and price and shows the total price. Changing quantity updates the total.
Notes: Can quantity go to zero or negative? What is the maximum quantity? Does the basket stay after logout?
