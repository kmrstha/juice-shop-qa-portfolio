QUESTIONS AND POTENTIAL DEFECTS

Q-01: The password advice lists five rules but "12345" and "abcdefgh" are accepted, so none of the advice is enforced. Which is intended: the advice, the 5-40 character message or both? (BUG-01, REQ-REG-12)

Q-02: The password field stops input at 40 characters but the repeat password field accepts more. With 41 characters typed in both, "Passwords do not match" is shown and registration is blocked. (BUG-02, REQ-REG-07)

Q-03: Does the server reject a password longer than 40 characters when it is sent directly without the browser? To test with postman in the API testing project.

Q-04: The password counter shows "10/20" at 10 characters and "40/20" at 40. The limit shown (20) does not match the allowed maximum (40). The denominator never changes. (BUG-03, REQ-REG-18) Open point: [does the repeat password field show a counter and what limit does it show?]

Q-05: After 6th consecutive failed logins, no lockout, delay or warning appeared. Is lockout or rate limiting intended? Without it, passwords can be guessed by repeated attempts. (Security observation, no requirement yet)

OBSERVATIONS
- The password advice says "lower character" and "upper character" instead of "lowercase letter" and "uppercase letter".
- The password counter appears only after 5 characters are entered.
- The same message, "Invalid email or password", is shown for an unregistered email and a wrong password. This does not reveal which emails exist.
